## Characteristics
Since the purpose of Resilience Councils is to democratize and decentralize decision-making, three characteristics seem essential:
- Independence: The Council operates autonomously and formulates its decisions independently of existing decision-making bodies.
- Broad representation: The Council represents a broader constituency rather than only the views and interests of its own members. Ideally, the Council represents societal groups that were previously not included in decision-making.
- Enlarging social democratic resilience: The Council preserves the autonomy of the perspectives entering it, makes perspectives that would otherwise remain inaccessible available to others, and connects those perspectives to consequential collective reflection and action.

## Criteria
This means that SAUFEX Resilience Councils could be evaluated according to the following criteria:
- Are the members of the Council independent of the current decision-makers, and do they formulate their decisions autonomously?
- Does the Council represent societal groups that are not otherwise included in decision-making?
- Does the Council enable its members to constitute and articulate their own perspectives rather than merely reproduce the categories, expectations, or interests of existing decision-makers?
- Does the Council actually expose collective decision-making to autonomous perspectives that would otherwise remain inaccessible?
- Are the recommendations by the Council consequential for collective decision-making?
